# YSJ Studio × JINZA — Official Brand Architecture

Source: owner's official brand document (received 2026-10-19). These rules
govern all website copy, checkout, receipts, customer communications, and
legal/business disclosures for the beat store.

## Business Name

**YSJ Studio**

## Artist and Producer Name

**JINZA**

## Business Description

YSJ Studio is an independent music production business specializing in
original instrumental production and beat licensing. JINZA is the artist and
producer identity behind its music catalog.

## Brand Structure

- **YSJ Studio** — the business name. Used for the website, business
  registration, customer service, order management, invoices, and commercial
  operations.
- **JINZA** — the artist identity. Used for producer branding, music
  releases, beat credits, social media content, and artistic promotion.
- **YSJ Studio website** — the official storefront where customers discover,
  license, and purchase beats produced by JINZA.

## Website Branding Requirements

Use **YSJ Studio** as the primary business identity in:

- Website header and footer
- Checkout experience
- Customer communications
- Legal/business information

Feature **JINZA** prominently as the producer and artist behind the catalog.

### Homepage headline

> **YSJ Studio**
> *Original production. Your next record.*

Supporting copy:

> "Discover and license original beats produced by JINZA. Explore multiple
> genres, find your sound, and bring your next record to life."

### Producer credit

> "Produced by JINZA | YSJ Studio"

## E-commerce and Legal Identity

- Use **YSJ Studio** as the storefront's business name.
- Configure Stripe, PayPal, receipts, customer support, and applicable legal
  disclosures with the correct business and payment-account information.
- **Do not assume YSJ Studio is a registered legal entity, LLC, or
  corporation.** Use the legal business name and business structure the
  owner provides when configuring payment providers, taxes, invoices, and
  legal documents.
- **JINZA is the producer credit and artist brand.** Do not mistakenly
  identify JINZA as the registered business name.

## Consistency Rule

Keep these identities consistent throughout the website while making their
relationship clear to customers: YSJ Studio is the business; JINZA is the
artist behind the music.

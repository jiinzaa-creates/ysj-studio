What we don't collect

How we use it

Your contact-form details are used only to reply to your inquiry and to provide the service you asked about. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except the email delivery service that carries your message to us.

Your rights

Ask us to correct or delete your contact information any time via the contact page. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your inquiry or the service it relates to.

Changes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft — not yet in effect.

What we collect

  • Order information: your email address and purchase details, needed to deliver your files and license.
  • Payment information: processed directly by Stripe or PayPal. YSJ Studio never sees or stores your card number.
  • On-device preferences: your theme choice and crate (cart) are stored in your own browser's local storage — they never leave your device until you check out.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no tracking pixels, no advertising profiles.
  • No card numbers or security codes, ever.

How we use it

Your email is used to deliver your purchased files and license, send order confirmations, and provide support. We don't sell your information.

Your rights

Ask us to correct or delete your order information any time via the contact page. Download records are kept for fraud prevention and support.

Changes

This is a draft. The final policy will be published before the store accepts payments.

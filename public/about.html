JINZA

JINZA is a Los Angeles-based multi-genre producer making original instrumentals for independent artists — dark trap, melodic R&B, rage, and everything in between.

His beats are available to license on TrackTrain, and now direct through YSJ Studio — the business behind the catalog. Every beat here is 100% owned and original, cleared in one stop.

On the business side, JINZA holds both ASCAP writer's and publisher's affiliations, keeping full control of his publishing.

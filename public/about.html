✦ The Producer ✦

JINZA

JINZA is a Los Angeles-based music producer with Arkansas roots, producing for seven years — and he'll work on anything. No genre limits, no lane.

He built YSJ Studio in Los Angeles as the company around the music: a music production company with administrative services. Production comes first — custom records, sync and commercial licensing, and engineering — and the business side comes with it: publishing administration and licensing administration, handled in-house by the same studio that made the record.

Every production is 100% owned and original, cleared in one stop. On the business side, JINZA holds both ASCAP writer's and publisher's affiliations, keeping full control of his publishing, and is a Symphonic Distribution client. His royalty affiliations run through ASCAP, The MLC, and SoundExchange.
